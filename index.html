Beginner tennis lessons in Newmarket

Learn at your pace.

Alex Lawrence offers friendly beginner tennis coaching in Newmarket, Suffolk, for adults who are new to tennis or want to build confidence with the basics. Come on your own for a private lesson or learn with someone else. Lessons are planned to begin in spring 2027, subject to the new public courts opening and court availability.

Meet Alex

I’ve been playing tennis for over 10 years and have played competitively across Cambridgeshire. I want beginners to discover how enjoyable tennis can be when it’s taught clearly and without pressure, with lessons focused on building confidence, good habits and a genuine feel for the game.

FAQs

A few useful details.

What happens during my first tennis lesson?

We’ll start by talking about your experience and what you’d like to get out of tennis. From there, we’ll work at a comfortable pace, focusing on the basics that are most useful for you. You don’t need any previous experience, and there’s plenty of time to ask questions.

Who are the lessons for?

They’re aimed mainly at adult beginners: whether you are completely new to tennis or want to build confidence with the fundamentals.

How long is a lesson?

Each regular lesson lasts one hour. Taster sessions are 45 minutes.

Can I learn with someone else?

Yes. Shared lessons are available for two people at £35 total for the hour.

What if I don’t have a racket?

Alex can provide a racket if you need one. Just mention it when you arrange your lesson.
